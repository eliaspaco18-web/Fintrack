# FinTrack Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

People maintaining their own multi-currency financial records, including accounts, transactions, credit, budgets, assets and money owed to or by other people. The primary contexts are a desktop monthly review and mobile transaction recording. This is grounded in the existing repository and the owner's two redesign briefs, not a claim of completed user research.

## Product Purpose

Help a person understand recorded financial position, movements and commitments, and move naturally from summary to explanation, underlying records and action. Existing financial logic and data remain protected during design work.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed and capable. The owner explicitly prefers FinTrack's teal/green/neutrals with restrained gold, and Concept 12 FT Ligature. Refine this identity without restarting a logo exploration. The application must feel like a complete financial tool, not a sparse showcase.

## Anti-references

Generic grids of equivalent KPI cards, decorative donuts, gradients and excessive shadows, unrelated module colors, ungrounded AI advice, invented forecasts, fake bank synchronization, or empty space that pushes useful information out of reach. The supplied dashboard image is an intention reference, not a specification or source of supported features.

## Design Principles

1. Financial truth comes before persuasive appearance: unknown is not zero, a receivable is not available cash, and a transfer is not report income.
2. Use controlled density to shorten the path between an overview and its supporting records.
3. Every visible element must answer a financial question or enable a real action.
4. Preserve context during inspection and entry; use progressive disclosure for detail, not to hide essential facts.
5. Keep one coherent FinTrack identity across light, dark, desktop, tablet and mobile.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

The redesign targets WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, visible focus, color-independent financial states, reduced motion, readable exact amounts and 44 px touch controls. These are implementation acceptance requirements from the repository design plan, not a claim of certification.
